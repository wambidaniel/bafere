# BAFERE labelling codebook

Judge the text only. Assume you do not know who sent the message.

**SCAM**: the message tries, under a false or unverifiable pretext, to get the reader to do at least one of:

1. send money, airtime or a fee to a personal number or other unofficial channel;
2. reveal a PIN, one-time code, verification code or password;
3. open an unofficial link or call an unofficial number to claim, fix, unlock or verify something;
4. take an action that helps someone take over an account or line (switch off the phone, approve a prompt).

**LEGIT**: everything else, including messages that mention money, prizes, PINs, codes or security warnings, and ordinary requests between people who clearly know each other.

## Categories

| Scam type | Legitimate hard negative |
|---|---|
| S1 Mistaken-transfer reversal | L1 Transaction receipts and notices |
| S2 Fake prize or promotion | L2 Genuine promotions and rewards |
| S3 Impersonation of telco, regulator, bank or police | L3 Security and service notices |
| S4 Relative or friend in distress | L4 Personal money requests and messages |
| S5 Advance-fee loan, job, investment or sale | L5 Loan, job and admission messages |
| S6 Account block, verification or phishing link | L6 Account alerts |
